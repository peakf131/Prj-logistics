Questions

Before you begin

Do I need a web developer?

No. If you can add a block of text to your website, you can add Instant Quote. On Pro we do it for you.

Is the price on the website binding?

The customer sends a booking request with the price shown. Nothing is confirmed until you reply, so you can still decline or change the time.

Which websites does it work on?

Any website that lets you add HTML or an “embed” block, including WordPress, Wix and Squarespace.

Can I change my prices?

Yes, at any time. Send us the new prices and they are usually live the same day.

Who is behind COSO?

COSO Courier Solutions is a trading name of PRJ Logistics Ltd, registered in England and Wales and with the ICO (ZC282214). We can sign a data processing agreement with you.
