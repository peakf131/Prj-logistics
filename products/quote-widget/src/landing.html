Questions

Do I need a web developer?

No. If you can add a block of text to your website, you can add this. On Pro we do it for you.

Is the price on the website binding?

The customer sends a booking request with the price shown. Nothing is confirmed until you reply, so you can still say no or change the time.

Where do the bookings go?

To the email address you choose, with the customer's details and a reply button. The customer gets a copy with their reference.

Can I change my prices?

Yes, at any time. Send us the new prices and they are usually live the same day.

What about customer data?

We only use customer details to send you the booking. PRJ Logistics Ltd is registered with the ICO, and we can sign a data processing agreement with you.
