Questions

Do drivers pay anything?

No. Drivers never pay us. Companies pay a fee when a driver we found stays with them.

Am I employed or self-employed?

Most courier work in the UK is self-employed: you invoice the company, pay your own tax and National Insurance and claim your costs. Some partners offer employed roles too.

I don't have a van. Can I still start?

Yes. Some delivery partners provide the van, fuel and insurance in your day rate. It's a good way to learn the job first.
