Who we work for

Our vans are set up for the kind of urgent and regular work that local businesses send every week:

  • Printers and sign makers: finished jobs to customers and events, on deadline.
  • Furniture makers, joiners and kitchen fitters: finished pieces and materials, handled with care.
  • Garages and motor factors: urgent parts between branches, suppliers and workshops.
  • Builders' merchants and trade suppliers: trade stock and materials to site or to a customer.
  • Manufacturers and workshops: components, samples and equipment between sites.
  • Shops and online sellers: same-day orders and stock transfers.

Urgent jobs and regular runs

Urgent: call or message us, tell us what is going where, and we give you a price before we go. Collections run Monday to Friday, between 08:00 and 18:00. After 4pm we can often still collect, though same-day delivery is not guaranteed for late bookings.

Regular: if you send on fixed days or to the same places, we can plan a regular run with agreed rates, so you do not need a new quote every time.

Collection area: around Reading and Wokingham, roughly from Newbury to Maidenhead and Ascot. Delivery: anywhere up to 200 miles from Reading, including London, Heathrow, Oxford, Southampton, Bristol and Birmingham.

Our vans

Long wheelbase (LWB) and short wheelbase (SWB) vans, each taking up to approximately 1,100 kg: from a single urgent box to several pallets. Our vans have no tail lift, so pallets need a forklift, or enough people to unload by hand, at both ends.

Subcontract work for other couriers

Need extra van capacity, or cover for a route outside your area? We take overflow work from other courier and logistics companies and deliver under your instructions, with photo proof of delivery you can pass on to your customer.

Questions businesses ask

Can you collect from our supplier and deliver to us?

Yes, if the supplier is in our collection area around Reading and Wokingham. Give us both addresses and the contact on site, and we will arrange the rest.

What happens if a delivery cannot be made?

We call you straight away and agree what to do. Failed delivery and waiting time charges are listed in our terms, and if we cannot carry out your job at all, you get a full refund.

Do we need a contract?

No. You can book single jobs whenever you need them. For regular work we simply agree rates and invoice terms in writing, by email.
