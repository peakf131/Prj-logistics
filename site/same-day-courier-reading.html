What we can carry

We run long wheelbase (LWB) and short wheelbase (SWB) vans, each taking loads up to approximately 1,100 kg. That covers most same-day work from Reading:

  • Documents and parcels that need to arrive today, not tomorrow.
  • Parts and equipment for workshops, engineers and production lines that cannot wait.
  • Pallets and trade stock. Our vans have no tail lift, so a forklift, or enough people to unload by hand, is needed at both ends.
  • Furniture and large items for shops, makers and private customers.

Not sure which van you need? Tell us roughly what you are sending and how heavy it is, and we will pick the right vehicle. A rough weight is fine.

How booking a same-day courier in Reading works

  1. Ask for a price. Use the quote form on our home page, message us on WhatsApp or call. Collections run Monday to Friday, between 08:00 and 18:00.
  2. Accept your price. One clear, all-inclusive price, agreed before we go. Private customers pay by card through a secure link; businesses can ask to pay by invoice.
  3. We collect. Your driver arrives at the agreed time, checks the load and secures it for the journey.
  4. We deliver with proof. The van goes straight to the address. You can follow the job on our tracking page, and we photograph the delivery and record the name of the person who received it.

If you book later in the day, we can often still collect after 4pm, but same-day delivery is not guaranteed for late bookings. We will always tell you honestly before you accept.
