Our vans

We run long wheelbase (LWB) and short wheelbase (SWB) vans, each taking up to approximately 1,100 kg. The SWB is quick and flexible for urgent, medium-sized jobs; the LWB takes bulky and heavy loads, including pallets. Tell us roughly what you are sending and we will match the van to the load.

How it works

  1. Get a price with the quote form on our home page, on WhatsApp or by phone. Collections run Monday to Friday, between 08:00 and 18:00.
  2. Accept the price. It is all-inclusive and agreed before we go. Businesses can ask to pay by invoice.
  3. We collect and drive straight there. One van, one load, no transfers on the way.
  4. Proof of delivery. We photograph the delivery and record who received it, and you can follow the job on our tracking page.

Bookings after 4pm can often still be collected, but same-day delivery is not guaranteed for late bookings. We will tell you honestly before you accept.

Questions from Wokingham customers

How quickly can you collect?

It depends on where our vans are when you call. We tell you the collection time together with the price, so you can decide before you book.

Can you deliver a pallet to a house?

Yes, as long as someone can take it off the van. We have no tail lift, so a forklift, or enough people to unload by hand, is needed at the delivery address. The pallet stays with your goods.

Will the price change on the day?

No, as long as the job matches what you told us. The only possible extras, such as waiting over 20 minutes or an extra stop, are listed in our FAQ and terms.
