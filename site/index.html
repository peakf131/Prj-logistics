      Common questions

      The quick answers are below. See all questions →

      How quickly can you collect?

      For local jobs around Reading and Wokingham we can often collect within hours. Call us, message us on WhatsApp or email us for urgent requests, and we will tell you straight away what is possible. Collections after 4pm are possible, but we cannot guarantee delivery the same day.

      What can you carry?

      Anything that fits in one of our LWB or SWB vans: boxes, parcels, pallets, equipment and trade goods. Tell us the size and weight and we will pick the right van. Our vans have no tail lift, so pallets need a forklift (or loading by hand) at both ends.

      What can’t you carry?

      We do not carry hazardous or dangerous goods, including flammable liquids, fuel, gas cylinders, chemicals, corrosives, explosives and fireworks. We also cannot take weapons, ammunition, illegal or prohibited items, or anything that is unsafe to transport. Not sure about your load? Ask us before you book and we will tell you.

      Do you go outside Reading and Wokingham?

      We collect in our local area around Reading and Wokingham, roughly from Newbury to Maidenhead and Ascot and deliver up to 200 miles from Reading. Further away? Just ask. Send an enquiry or email us and we will tell you if we can help.

      When do I find out the price?

      Before we collect. We confirm the price with you first, so there are no surprises.

      Can I get regular deliveries?

      Of course. Businesses with repeat deliveries can arrange regular bookings. See Partnership above.
