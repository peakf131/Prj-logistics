Insurance

We carry the insurance our customers expect from a professional courier. Here is what it means for your delivery.

Goods in transit insurance

Cover for your goods while they are in our care, from collection to delivery.

Public liability insurance

Cover for injury to other people or damage to their property caused by our work.

Goods in transit insurance

PRJ Logistics Ltd holds goods in transit insurance. It is there to protect you if your goods are lost, stolen or damaged while we are carrying them. Cover applies from the moment we collect your load until it is delivered, subject to the terms, limits and exclusions of the policy.

Public liability insurance

We also hold public liability insurance. This covers claims from other people, such as injury or damage to property, that may arise from our activities, for example at a collection or delivery address.

Good to know before you book

  • Tell us what you are sending. The more we know about the load, its value and any special handling, the better we can look after it. Please tell us about high-value, fragile or unusual items before you book so we can confirm they are suitable.
  • Packing and securing. Goods need to be properly packed and ready for transport. Our drivers have many years of experience in securing loads, and they will secure your goods safely in the vehicle.
  • Policy terms apply. Cover is subject to the policy limits, excess and exclusions. Some types of goods may not be covered under our standard policy. If you are unsure, ask us before you book.
  • If something goes wrong. Tell us as soon as possible, and keep the packaging and any photos. Our proof of delivery photos and your reference number help us deal with a claim quickly.
